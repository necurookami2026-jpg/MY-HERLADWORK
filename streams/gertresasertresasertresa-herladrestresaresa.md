# Gertresasertresasertresa · Herladrestresaresa Stream

An All Recursive audience stream for deciding what to solve, trying a small change, and sharing what happened. **Strecturesasresa** is the problem-solving structure. **Nomic** is the audience process for proposing changes to that structure.

[Submit a problem or a rule proposal](https://github.com/necurookami2026-jpg/MY-HERLADWORK/issues/new?template=herald-stream.yml)

## Opening broadcast

> Bring one problem the audience can observe. Say who it affects, what happens now, and what a better outcome would look like. We’ll choose one problem, try one change, and report what we learned. You can also propose a change to the rules for the next round.

## Strecturesasresa stream

| Beat | What the herald asks | What the audience records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · Notice | What is happening, and who is affected? | A concrete observation and where it occurred |
| 02 · Frame | What should be possible? How will we tell? | One problem statement and one success check |
| 03 · Propose | What small change can we test first? | Candidate changes, trade-offs, and an owner for the trial |
| 04 · Test | What happened when we tried it? | The conditions, observed result, and anything that surprised us |
| 05 · Return | Should we keep the change, revise it, or try another? | The decision, its reason, and the next action |

Choose a problem for a round based on its effect on the audience and whether the group can test a change. Record the choice in its issue so someone joining later can see where the round stands.

## Nomic audience round

1. Select **Rule proposal** in the [audience form](https://github.com/necurookami2026-jpg/MY-HERLADWORK/issues/new?template=herald-stream.yml).
2. Name the rule or beat you want to change. Give the replacement wording, the reason, and a way to tell whether it helps.
3. Invite comments in the issue. At the end of the round, the facilitator records the decision and the version of the stream that the change applies to.
4. If adopted, apply the rule starting with the next round. Keep the original rule and decision visible in the issue.

## Example round

**Problem.** Readers of an institute profile have trouble finding its Restresa image.

**Trial.** Add a “View the flood image” link near the profile heading. Ask five readers to find the image from the top of the page without a hint.

**Success check.** At least four readers reach the image within 30 seconds. Record the count and any point where readers got stuck.

**Report back.** Share the result in the issue, then decide whether to keep the link, change its wording, or test a different placement. This example describes a proposed trial.

## Stream rules

**Version 1.** Work on one clearly framed problem per round. Record evidence separately from ideas. Report the result before closing the round. Rule changes apply from the next round onward.
